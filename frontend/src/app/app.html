DOES A DEGREE PROTECT YOU?

In most countries a degree lowers unemployment — but not everywhere. In Haiti, Ecuador, Nicaragua, Bolivia and Mexico, graduates are more likely to be unemployed than the average worker.

Source: World Bank, SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS and SL.UEM.ADVN.ZS (latest year).
